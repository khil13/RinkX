# 06 · UI Structure & Wireframes

## Design language: "analytics terminal"

* **Dark-first.** Background `#0B0F14` and panels `#121821`, with 1 px `#1F2A37` hairlines and slate text. One accent (ice blue `#4FB3FF`). Semantic colors: Over `#22C55E`, Under `#F97316`, Neutral `#94A3B8`, Warning `#EAB308`. **Color is never the only signal**: every color is paired with a ▲/▼ icon or a text label.
* **Typography.** Inter for UI text, JetBrains Mono with `tabular-nums` for every number, so columns align.
* **Density.** Compact 32 px table rows on desktop. Cards on mobile. Sticky headers, keyboard navigation (`/` focuses search, `j/k` moves rows).
* **Data-state chips** on every panel: `LIVE · 2m ago`, `STALE · 47m`, `UNAVAILABLE`, `SYNTHETIC`. A synthetic-data banner sits fixed at the top in dev.
* **Banned copy:** "lock", "guaranteed", "free money", "can't lose", "sure thing", "hammer". Enforced by a CI lint.

## Navigation

Every page header shows **"Updated N min ago"** from the manifest, plus a refresh hint linking to *Run workflow* when data is stale.

The menu has nine sections; a section with more than one page shows its pages as tabs at the top (defined once in `web/src/nav.ts`):

```
Today · Card of the Day · Props (All props, Line movement) · Games · Players
Lineups (Goalies, Lines & PP, News & alerts) · Parlay
Results (Performance, Backtest, My bets, Model tests) · Settings (Settings, Data & admin)
```

Desktop: a left rail with the sections.
Mobile: a bottom tab bar (Today · Card · Props · Parlay · More), with the parlay's leg count on its tab. "More" opens a bottom sheet with the other sections and Lock. It closes on Escape or a tap outside. On Props, the filters sit in a bottom sheet behind a "Filters" button (it shows how many are on), so the props come first. On Today, "By market", "Daily report" and "Data feeds" start folded on a phone; tap a title to open it.

Today is the home page. When no data source is connected it says so and lists each feed; otherwise it lists only feeds that failed or went stale. Admin always shows every feed.

### Card of the Day
`/props/best`. The strongest props for one date, in team colours.
- **The date's games only.** The date is the NHL slate date (Eastern time) unless a later date tab is picked; the page never jumps to another day on its own. A pick must be dated that day *and* be one of the games in that date's published slate (`slate/{date}.json`). A strip at the top lists those games with team chips, start time and how many picks each has (or "no lines yet" / "started").
- **Team props** (moneyline, totals): one pick per game, ranked by EV, up to 4. **Player props** are grouped by market in a fixed order (shots on goal, points, goals, assists, blocks, hits, goalies), up to 3 per group by EV, one pick per player across the card. Grouping keeps one market (longshot goal props have the biggest EV) from filling every slot. Only lines with a lean count, each at the best-priced book. Shots on goal, points and goals always show; an empty one says whether no line was posted for that date or how many were priced without clearing the bar.
- **Team colours.** Each card has a left stripe in the team's primary and secondary colours (split between both teams for a game total) and a team chip whose text is black or white, whichever has more WCAG contrast. All 32 NHL abbreviations are mapped. Unknown abbreviations, such as synthetic teams, use a neutral grey. These are colours only: no logos or marks.
- Empty sections say so ("nothing clears the bar on this date"). Nothing is forced onto the card. Tapping a pick opens the same prop card as Props.

### Prop card: scores and explanations
* **Prop Intelligence** badge on each card ("🔥 94/100" at 80+). A **Why?** button shows every part, its weight and its points.
* **Shot Environment** on shots-on-goal props, with its three biggest factors.
* **Projection vs line** on each card ("proj 3.80 (+1.30)").
* **Why this prop**: the inputs as bullets, plus a short summary written only from them.
* **Model confidence vs prop value**, side by side, with why they differ.
* **View calculation** on projections: the base projection plus each factor as an additive step, ending at the projection.
* Line movement per book: open → now, in cents ("📈 20-cent move"), and line changes ("2.5 → 3.5").

### Backtest (`/backtest`)
This replays RinkX's own graded, pre-game prices from the last 365 days (`backtest.json`): for each prop and
book, the last price frozen before puck drop.

* **Filters:** market, line, min model probability, min edge, min Prop Intelligence, book, side, leans only.
  All of them are values frozen before the game, so nothing looks ahead.
* **Bets:** one bet per prop at the best price that passes, at 1 unit each.
* **Results:** bets, W–L–P, win %, units, ROI, average odds, average CLV in cents, share that beat the close,
  and maximum drawdown. Fewer than 100 bets carries a warning.
* **Closing-line value:** grouped by market, player, sportsbook or model version.

### My performance (`/my`)
"I placed this" on any prop card, and under each card in the Props list, records the bet on this device only, at the shown price and book, 1 unit, in one tap. Once placed, the price and units can be corrected in place (on the prop card), or the entry undone.

* Results and closing prices come from RinkX's grading.
* The page shows win %, ROI, units, CLV, average odds, and bets with W–L–P.
* It filters to 7 days, 30 days or the season, by when the bet was tracked.
* It breaks results down by SOG / goals / points / assists / hits / blocks.

### Parlay builder from the slate
Choose a market, a number of legs (2–8) and a risk level, or press **🔥 BUILD MY 8-LEG** (Balanced, every
market, today's slate). Each risk level has stated rules:

* **Conservative:** model probability ≥ 58%, edge ≥ 3 pts, Prop Intelligence ≥ 65, confidence ≥ 60.
* **Balanced:** ≥ 50%, ≥ 3 pts, ≥ 55, ≥ 50.
* **Aggressive:** ≥ 30%, ≥ 4 pts, ≥ 45, ≥ 40.
* **All levels:** at most 2 legs per game (3 for Aggressive), and no price that moved sharply against the leg.

A greedy search starts from the best single leg and, at each step, adds the leg that most improves the risk
level's objective for the whole parlay, counting correlations:

* Conservative: combined probability with positive EV.
* Balanced: EV × √probability.
* Aggressive: EV.

So it isn't the eight highest-rated props: a leg that moves with others, or crowds one game, can lose its
place. Each leg shows line, odds, model and market probability, edge and Prop Intelligence. The combined
probability is shown too. If too few legs qualify, the builder stops short and says so. Correlations are
labelled Positive / Negative only when the estimate's 95% interval excludes zero (otherwise "No clear
relationship").

### Lines & power play (`/deployment`)
Each skater's line and PP unit for upcoming games: his last game's (from the shift chart), or a Quick Entry
line change with its source. The page shows expected vs last-5 TOI, and badges for verified changes against
his previous game: 🔥 MOVED TO PP1, 🔥 PROMOTED TO TOP LINE, ⚠️ DROPPED FROM PP1, ⚠️ DROPPED FROM TOP LINE,
⚠️ EXPECTED TOI DOWN (2+ min below his last 5), 🔥 EXPECTED TOI UP. Projections use the same deployment, so a
change moves them automatically.

### Player prop profile
On every skater page:
* **Shooting**: SOG/game, SOG/60, shot attempts, share of team SOG, last 5/10, home/away, recent SOG.
* **Usage**: TOI season / last 5, PP TOI, and the last five games' line/PP from shift charts.
* **Matchup**: next opponent, its SOG allowed vs league, and its SOG allowed to his position over the last 20 games.
* **Props**: current lines with projection, model probability, edge and last-10 hit rate.
* **Shot map**: from play-by-play coordinates, filtered by last 5, last 10, season or vs an opponent. On goal,
  goals, missed/blocked, RinkX's high-danger definition, and a density view.

Goalies show their season numbers on every starter line: starts, SV% vs league, last-5 SV%, SA and GA per
start, and starts in the last 7 days. Advanced metrics show as unavailable: no xG source is connected.

### Today (`/today`)
The daily intelligence page for the NHL slate date, with a market filter. It has these sections:
* Top 10 props by Prop Intelligence;
* best props by market (SOG, points, goals, assists, blocks, hits, goalie);
* biggest projection edges and biggest line moves (in cents);
* injury/lineup alerts with sources;
* starting goalies.

The **Daily report** below them has these sections: best props, best SOG/goal/point/assist props, biggest
market moves, lineup news, value opportunities and red flags. Each line restates a published number, and an
empty section says so. "Copy text" copies the report. Public betting percentages show **DATA UNAVAILABLE**:
no licensed source is connected, and nothing is estimated.

### Game prop center
On each upcoming game, there's the best prop per market group at the best book. It can be sorted by Prop
Intelligence, model edge, projection vs line, last-10 hit rate, odds or EV.

### Freshness
Pages say "Updated N min ago · source" and switch to "⚠️ DATA MAY BE STALE" once data is older than expected.
Missing data shows "DATA UNAVAILABLE" or "INSUFFICIENT DATA", never a guess.

### Other pages
* **Players / profile:** a header with bio and role chips. Tabs: Overview (season stats, splits) · Game Log · Usage (TOI trend, PP share, line history timeline) · Props (projection vs. actual history per market) · Shots (rink shot map, if PBP exists) · Matchups (vs. opponents).
* **Goalies** *(built in Phase 10)*: today's and tomorrow's starters with status (confirmed with source, projected with share, or from the box score), a count of how many are confirmed, and a Quick Entry link to confirm a projected one. Saves projections stay on the game and player pages.
* **Line Movement** *(built in Phase 10)*: every open main line for upcoming games, first price to now, sorted by the biggest change in implied probability. It has a "moved only" toggle and a market filter. Each row links to the player page, where the per-book step chart is, with markers for goalie confirmations, availability changes, news and injury changes.
* **Parlay Builder** *(built in Phase 8)*: legs added from a prop card ("Add to parlay"), kept on the device.
  - Probability if independent and adjusted for correlation, fair odds, the legs' prices multiplied with EV, and a permanent variance warning.
  - A list of leg pairs with each ρ, its 95% interval and n, or why the pair is treated as independent. This replaced the heatmap: with a handful of legs a list is easier to read.
  - Legs for games that have started are flagged.
* **Model Performance** *(built in Phase 7)*: graded live-tracked results.
  - Summary tiles: bets with W-L-P, profit, ROI with its 95% range, and CLV with % beating the close.
  - A cumulative profit chart with the worst drawdown, and a reliability diagram with Brier and log loss next to the no-vig market's.
  - Tables by market, confidence bucket (with the monotonic check), month and model version, then the 50 most recent graded bets with their void reasons.
  - Below 100 bets a warning says the sample is too small. Before any game is graded, it says so.
  - The walk-forward (`lineup_oracle`) view stays on Model Tests, linked from the page rather than toggled.
* **News & alerts** *(built in Phase 8)*: the news feed (category chip, headline linking to the source, source host and reliability, published time, player and team) with a category filter, and the alerts section (configured alerts, fired events with delivery status). Player pages show that player's news. `/alerts` is folded into this page.
* **Admin:** source health grid, job runs with failures, market coverage matrix, data-quality queue, model registry, and **Quick Entry** buttons that open a prefilled GitHub Issue form (goalie / scratch / line / injury, with the game and player already filled in).

## Mobile (iPhone) requirements
* Designed at 390 × 844 first, with a 16 px gutter, 44 px minimum tap targets, and safe-area insets for the bottom tab bar.
* Tables become cards: player and prop on top, then line/projection/edge/confidence, and hit rate in a footer row. Horizontal scroll is only allowed inside the line-comparison table, with a sticky first column.
* Bottom-sheet drawers: on a phone the prop card opens as a sheet anchored to the bottom edge, and so does "More". Sheets slide in unless the phone asks for reduced motion. *(Phase 9.)* Explain views stay inline (they expand in place). Pull-to-refresh is left to the browser: the manifest is re-checked when the app comes back into focus.
* **Home-screen app** *(Phase 9)*:
  - a web manifest with PNG icons (192, 512, maskable 512) and an Apple touch icon, plus `apple-mobile-web-app-*` tags;
  - a standalone display with safe-area padding at the top and bottom.
  - The service worker (`public/sw.js`) is **network first** for every request. Its cache is only used when the phone is offline, so an online visit never shows an old app or old data, and cached data stays encrypted.
  - Alerts arrive through the ntfy app, since Pages can't send Web Push without a server.
* **Settings** *(Phase 9, on the device only)*:
  - odds format: American or decimal;
  - time zone: the device's, or Eastern, Central, Mountain, Pacific or UTC;
  - **cool-off**: 24 h, 3 days, 1 week or 30 days. It hides Props, Card of the Day, the parlay builder and every sportsbook price on this device. Stats, games and model tests stay available. The app can extend a cool-off but not end it early.
* **Unlock screen:** one passphrase field and a "Remember on this device" toggle. Wrong passphrases fail the AES-GCM tag check, and nothing partial is shown.
* Performance budget: LCP < 2.5 s on 4G, JS < 200 kB gzipped on the initial route.
  - *As built (Phase 9):* every signed-in page is loaded on demand, so the unlock screen downloads about 99 kB of gzipped JS (the React runtime, router, data layer and crypto).
  - `npm run build` (and CI) fails if the entry JS goes over 135 kB, all JS over 200 kB, or CSS over 12 kB, all gzipped.
  - Lighthouse mobile (simulated slow 4G) on the unlock screen: performance 91, accessibility 100, best practices 100. LCP is about 3 s under that throttling; on a real 4G phone it is well under the 2.5 s target, but the simulated number is the honest one to quote. Signed-in pages need the passphrase, so Lighthouse can't measure them; the Playwright suite runs every page on the iPhone 15 profile instead.
